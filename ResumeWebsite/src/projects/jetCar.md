# EDF Powered RC Car

My group built this totally rad RC jet car as the final project for our 300-level mechanical engineering design course. We were prescribed a pretty beefy ducted fan, oversized battery, and told to build a race car. Winner's of the race at the end of the semester got higher grades. We got 2nd place out of eight teams, so not a bad showing. I think we built the best car, though. I mean, check out that F1 style suspension I designed!
